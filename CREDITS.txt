RPHELPER FOREVER CREDITS

RPHelper Forever is a new implementation inspired by the historical
RoleplayingHelper addon family. It retains elements of the historical reaction
catalogue while replacing the runtime, user interface, configuration, and
persistence implementation.

Documented lineage

- Talyn is identified as the original RoleplayingHelper author in recovered
  historical metadata and in the later TurtleRoleplayingHelper repository.
- Overlord-Q is credited alongside Talyn as an original developer in the
  RPHelper 2 distribution metadata and later project acknowledgements.
- mithyk is credited for many default reactions in RPHelper 2 and in historical
  generic, race, Rogue, and random-insult source headers.
- Syrsa is credited in historical generic and several race-content headers.
- crashinbrn is credited on a preserved Rogue reaction.
- Duerma authored and distributed the second-generation RPHelper 2 release.
- Privelgus is credited for German translations in RPHelper 2. Older historical
  material also credits ArminAce and Isjari for translation or related help;
  those contributions are not identified as part of the current English addon.
- Vaaslite maintained a documented community adaptation for Turtle WoW.
- Wiubiki maintained the later RPhelper_twow adaptation and created the new
  RPHelper Forever implementation.

The surviving historical record is incomplete and not fully consistent. In
particular, some original contribution boundaries and the spelling “mithyk”
versus “mythik” remain uncertain. The statements above report the attributions
found in the cited sources; they do not determine ownership, claim permission,
or imply endorsement of RPHelper Forever by any historical contributor.
